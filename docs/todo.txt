This is the single list of what to work on next. Whoever picks up the project (a person,
Claude, Codex or any other coding agent) should be able to start from this file without
the earlier conversations. `AGENTS.md` at the repository root says how to keep it current

`docs/todo_archive.txt`.

Status tags used below:

  [next]         pick these up first, top to bottom
  [in progress]  started; the note says where it stopped

  [planned]      agreed, not started
  [parked]       on hold on purpose; do not start without asking the owner
  [idea]         not agreed yet; raise it with the owner before building

When you finish an item, move it to Recently Completed with the date and commit, then
archive older Recently Completed entries at the next milestone.

- [planned] Ignore single odd sales in changes and lists (agreed 2026-10-17; start after the
  Pullbacks review ~2026-10-24, ideally once the feed gap is behind us, mid-November).
  Problem: TCGplayer's market price averages recent sales, so one junk sale (damaged copy,
  fake, or a graded card sold as raw) can hold it far off for months on rarely sold cards.
  Venusaur 1st Ed. (Base Shadowless 015/102): a ~$72 sale on 2025-08-14 held the price at
  $72-75 until 2026-07-31 (listings $750+), so it shows +1,246% over 90 days. Entei Star (EX
  Unseen Forces 113/115): a 99-cent sale held it at $0.99 2026-05-06 to 08-01 (listing $1,600);
  +107,678% over 90 days, and today's $1,067 still averages the 99 cents in.
  Rule tested 2026-10-17 (scratchpad trial, two years of English prices): a new price level is
  ignored (hold the last good price) when it is under 40% or over 250% of the card's normal
  price (median of its last 10 accepted levels) AND listings don't back it on at least half
  its days (drop: lowPrice <= 2x the level; spike: midPrice >= half the level). Do not use
  "the next level confirms it": averaged wobbles around the junk price fooled that.
  Result: 1,705 items affected in the last year (77 at $100+), 324 today (58 at $20+);
  no store-priced SKU affected.
  Owner's choice: keep showing TCGplayer's price as is; use the cleaned series only for the
  % changes, indicators, lists and the 6-Month Hold. Build it as a nightly step before the
  snapshots, keep the raw prices, show Venusaur's and Entei's charts before/after first.

Profiling Notes (April 2026)
----------------------------

- Container timings for the default card dashboard: `/series` ~472 ms, `/health` ~227 ms,
  `/top_movers` ~169 ms, `/sma30_holds` ~121 ms.
- A URL-selected `early_uptrends` view: `/early_uptrends` ~2.19 s, `/series` ~1.55 s,
  `/universe?limit=1` ~1.35 s, `/health` ~1.27 s.
- Suspects worth reducing: first-paint dependence on `/health`; `/universe?limit=1` fallback
  hits when the selected row already has enough data; heavy non-default endpoints blocking
  the first screen when they come from URL state.

Future Ideas
------------

- Keep moving expensive live SQL into precomputed snapshots.
- Load independent sections in parallel where it actually improves perceived speed.
- Lightweight caching where safe: health, group signals, top movers, universe metadata.
- Keep detailed card/set data loading on selection; keep mini-graph payloads small and
  snapshot-backed.
- Buy List as a card-show sourcing workflow with targets and shopping-list behavior.
- A flip-focused basket using `recent_distinct_prices_30d`, ROC windows, rarity buckets and
  spend caps.
- Market Pulse facts backed by current snapshots rather than editorial filler.
- Budget, collection-completion and entry/exit assistant prompts ("what looks buyable now",
  "what is flattening out", "what may have reached a resale target").
- A pre-breakout accumulation screen.
