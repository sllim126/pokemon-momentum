Use 0 mm when you print and cut at home: one cut between cards. Print shops usually ask for 3 mm.

Printing tips
  • Print at 100% or "Actual size". "Fit to page" shrinks the cards. One card should measure 63 × 88 mm.
  • Matte cardstock around 250–300 gsm (90–110 lb) feels close to a real card and slides into a sleeve.
  • Cut on the marks with a paper trimmer, sleeve each card, then fill the pockets row by row, left to right.
  • Below 150 DPI the print looks soft. Zooming in lowers the DPI.
