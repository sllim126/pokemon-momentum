1. The price list

Paste one of these, then Read the text:

  • A whole shop page (e.g. MACHO TCG's list): scroll to the bottom so every product has loaded, then Ctrl+A, Ctrl+C. Each product's sealed box price is used; the other versions, stock and barcode go in its note.
  • A list: one item per line, its yen price at the end ("Mega Dream ¥12,800").
  • A screenshot (Messenger): choose it below and its text is read into the box. Check it: the reader can mistake a digit, and it can't read Japanese names.
