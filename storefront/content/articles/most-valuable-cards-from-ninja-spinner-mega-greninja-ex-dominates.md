## **Ninja Spinner is Here — Card Values, Chaos Rising Details, and More!**

Ninja Spinner has officially launched in Japan, and with it comes a flood of card reveals, early price data, and confirmation of what English players can expect when **Chaos Rising** drops on **May 22nd**. Here's everything you need to know.

### Early Card Prices: Mega Greninja ex Dominates

As expected, the set's gold card — **#122 Mega Greninja ex** — takes the top spot at **¥94,800 (~$593)**, consistent with previous gold cards from main sets.

The real surprise is the **#2 spot**: rather than a Supporter card, it's Mega Greninja ex's **Special Illustration Rare (#116)** at **¥39,800 (~$250)** — well ahead of every other secret rare. This is likely driven by Greninja's massive fanbase and the fact that its artwork connects beautifully with the Illustration Rares of Froakie (#088) and Frogadier (#089), all illustrated by **Susumu Maeya**.

Even Mega Greninja ex's **full art (#100)** is holding its own, sitting around **$21** — on par with the SIRs of Roxie's Performance, Mega Floette ex, and Cinccino ex. That's a remarkable showing for a full art.

Other notable prices at a glance:

- **Mega Dragalge ex SIR** — ¥2,980 (~$18.77)
- **AZ's Tranquility SIR** — ¥1,480 (~$9.32)
- **Special Red Card FA** — ¥1,280 (~$8.06)
- **Froakie IR** — ¥1,280 (~$8) *(top-ranked Illustration Rare, double the others)*

Among Double Rares, **Mega Greninja ex** stands out at **$4** and **Beedrill ex** at **$1.50**, likely reflecting their competitive playability. Most other Double Rares sit at 75 cents or less.

### 🇺🇸 Chaos Rising: What English Players Get

**Chaos Rising** will be an almost direct translation of Ninja Spinner, arriving as our **fourth English Mega Evolution set**. Here's the confirmed breakdown:

- **122 cards** total
- **5 Mega Evolution Pokémon ex**: Mega Pyroar ex, Mega Greninja ex, Mega Floette ex, Mega Dragalge ex, and likely Mega Gallade ex (promo)
- **5 Pokémon ex**: Beedrill ex, Gourgeist ex, Cobalion ex, Cinccino ex, and likely Krookodile ex (Gym promo)
- **11 Illustration Rare Pokémon**
- **18 Ultra Rare Pokémon and Trainer cards**
- **6 Special Illustration Rares**
- **20+ Trainer cards**

The **Elite Trainer Box promo** will be **Fennekin's Illustration Rare** (#085), cut from the main set. Froakie's IR was likely skipped since it forms a connected triptych with Frogadier and Mega Greninja ex — pulling it would leave the other two incomplete.

Like *Phantasmal Flames* and *Perfect Order*, Chaos Rising will be one of the **smallest English sets since 2010**, a trend likely tied to global TCG printing shortages.

**Prereleases** run **May 9th–17th**. Preorders on [PokemonCenter.com](http://PokemonCenter.com) aren't live yet, so keep watch on Twitter or your preferred Discord servers for notifications.

### Connected Artwork to Watch For

If you're chasing the connected artwork trio, you'll want all three of these:

- **#088 Froakie** (Illustration Rare)
- **#089 Frogadier** (Illustration Rare)
- **#116 Mega Greninja ex** (Special Illustration Rare)
