## Munikis Zero Officially Revealed – Mega Zygarde ex Arrives January 23

A brand-new Japanese Pokémon TCG expansion, **Munikis Zero**, has been officially revealed during Japan’s annual December livestream, with a confirmed release date of **January 23, 2026**. The announcement was first detailed by PokeBeach and immediately sparked major discussion across the competitive and collector communities.

### Headliner: Mega Zygarde ex

Mega Zygarde ex debuts as a **Basic Fighting-type Pokémon with a massive 310 HP**, an eye-catching stat that has already become one of the most talked-about aspects of the set.

**Key attacks include:**

- **Gaia Wave** – 200 damage with built-in damage reduction next turn
- **Munikis Zero** – A devastating multi-target attack that can hit *each* of your opponent’s Pokémon for 150 damage per successful coin flip

As a Mega Evolution Pokémon ex, it awards **3 Prize cards when Knocked Out**, reinforcing the high-risk, high-reward playstyle.

### Meta-Shaping Support Cards

Several newly revealed cards hint at major shifts in gameplay:

- **Meowth ex** – A Colorless Pokémon ex that searches your deck for a Supporter when benched, drawing immediate comparisons to Lumineon V
- **Core Memory (Pokémon Tool)** – Grants Mega Zygarde ex access to *Geobuster*, a 350-damage nuke
- **Lumiose City (Stadium)** – Allows players to bench a Basic Pokémon but immediately ends their turn
- **Rock Fighting Energy** – Prevents *all effects* of attacks against attached Fighting Pokémon

Community reaction has been especially strong around Meowth ex, with many predicting it will become a staple consistency engine.

### Mega Gallade ex Special Set

Launching **alongside Munikis Zero on January 23**, the **Mega Gallade ex Special Set** introduces a Stage 2 Mega Evolution with a staggering **350 HP** and powerful synergy with self-damage strategies.

### Set Size & English Implications

Munikis Zero contains **only 80 cards (before secret rares)**, suggesting it will likely be combined with **Ninja Spinner** cards when adapted into English. The English release, currently titled **Perfect Order**, is scheduled for **March 27, 2026**.

### Upcoming Japanese TCG Schedule

- **Jan 23** – Munikis Zero (Mega Zygarde ex)
- **Jan 23** – Mega Gallade ex Special Set
- **Mar 13** – Ninja Spinner (Mega Greninja ex)
- **TBA** – Storm Emeralda (Mega Rayquaza ex)
- **TBA** – Abyss Eye (Mega Darkrai ex)
- **TBA** – Celebration Collection (30th Anniversary set)

This set is shaping up to be a **major turning point** for Fighting-type decks and Mega Evolution design moving forward.

We’ll be tracking **Japanese pre-orders, sealed booster boxes, and future English translations** closely. Updates will be posted to **Poke6s.com** as product availability and pricing become clear.
