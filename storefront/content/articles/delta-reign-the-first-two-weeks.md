Delta Reign came out on November 6. Every new set raises the same question: do you buy the cards you want now, or wait for prices to settle?

I have daily prices for every Mega Evolution set since release, so instead of guessing, I lined Delta Reign up against the last five sets on the same days after release.

*Prices are TCGplayer market prices through November 20. "Master set" means the market price of every printing in the set added together, the same method as [Master Sets, Part 2](/articles/master-sets-part-2-japanese-vs-english).*

## Delta Reign so far

The whole master set was worth **$1,121** on release day. Two weeks later it's **$934**, down **17%**.

The big cards:

- **Mega Rayquaza ex** (Special Illustration Rare, 138/103): $328 → **$292** (−11%)
- **Mega Rayquaza ex** (Mega Hyper Rare, 141/103): $349 → **$220** (−37%)
- **Raikou ex** (Special Illustration Rare, 136/103): $82 → **$100** (+23%)
- **Kecleon** (Illustration Rare, 117/103): $15 → **$24** (+63%)

So it's not all falling. The two Rayquazas came down, but a couple of cards people actually like the art on went *up*. Kecleon is up more than 60%. [ADAM: Kecleon joke here?]

## How it compares with the last five sets

Here's each set's master set value on day 14, compared with release day:

- **Phantasmal Flames:** −36%
- **Chaos Rising:** −34%
- **Pitch Black:** −30%
- **Delta Reign:** −17%
- **Mega Evolution:** −9%
- **Perfect Order:** +4%

Delta Reign is falling more slowly than most. But the first two weeks are just the start. Here's where those same sets were about **three months** after release:

- **Chaos Rising:** −62%
- **Pitch Black:** −60%
- **Mega Evolution:** −48%
- **Phantasmal Flames:** −44%
- **Perfect Order:** −17%

On average, a Mega Evolution set's master set lost **almost half its value in the first 90 days**. Even Perfect Order and Mega Evolution, which held up best through the first two weeks, dropped later.

## The chase cards fall the hardest

Top chase cards, release day vs about 90 days later:

- **Mega Darkrai ex** (Pitch Black): $381 → $135 (−65%)
- **Mega Greninja ex** (Chaos Rising): $480 → $175 (−64%)
- **Mega Lucario ex** (Mega Evolution): $587 → $327 (−44%)
- **Mega Charizard X ex** (Phantasmal Flames): $810 → $613 (−24%)

Charizard is the exception, as always. It climbed back to $830 about six months in, before settling at $529 today. Everything else just kept sliding.

## Sealed holds up better

Booster boxes, release day vs about 90 days later:

- **Chaos Rising:** $237 → $173 (−27%)
- **Mega Evolution:** $295 → $245 (−17%)
- **Pitch Black:** $208 → $182 (−13%)
- **Phantasmal Flames:** $287 → $275 (−4%)
- **Perfect Order:** $207 → $218 (+5%)

That's a much smaller drop than the singles. And the older ones have come back: when I wrote this, a Phantasmal Flames box was **$372** (30% above release), and Mega Evolution was right back at its release price of around $300. Here's where they are today:

## So, buy now or wait?

Based on the last five sets:

- **Want specific singles?** Waiting has paid off almost every time. If you're eyeing the Rayquaza SIR, the last five sets say it'll probably be cheaper in a couple of months. (Unless it's a Charizard. It's never a Charizard you get to wait on.)
- **Want to open packs?** Booster boxes drop a little in the first few months, then tend to hold or recover once the set gets harder to find.
- **Want the master set?** It'll likely cost a lot less by February than it does today. The bulk is cheap either way. It's the secret rares that come down.

None of this is a guarantee. Five sets is a small sample, and a set can always surprise us. But if you're feeling the "buy it before it's gone" pressure on Delta Reign singles, the data says you can probably relax.

I'll check back on Delta Reign at the three-month mark to see if it follows the pattern.
