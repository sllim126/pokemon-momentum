Every year around now, everyone's wondering if they should wait for Black Friday to buy that booster box or Elite Trainer Box. It's a fair question. Everything else goes on sale, so why not Pokémon sealed products (on the secondary market, of course)?

So, with the daily TCGplayer prices I have going back to early 2024, so I decided to check what actually happened to sealed products around the last two Black Fridays (November 29, 2024 and November 28, 2025).

*TCGplayer's market price is based on what things actually sold for, so if Black Friday made sealed product cheaper across the board, it would show up here. I compared each product's price two weeks before Black Friday with Black Friday itself and the six weeks after. English sealed products worth $20 or more: 879 products in 2024, 1,261 in 2025.*

## Short answer: no

For the typical sealed product, the price on Black Friday was about the same as two weeks earlier: **+0.1%** in 2024 and **+0.3%** in 2025. Then it kept going up.

**2024, the typical product:**

- Black Friday: +0.1%
- Two weeks later: +2%
- Six weeks later (mid-January): +6%

**2025, the typical product:**

- Black Friday: +0.3%
- Two weeks later: +2%
- Six weeks later: +4%

Here's the same thing day by day. The solid lines show the share of products priced higher than two weeks before, the dashed lines the share priced lower:

![Share of sealed products priced higher and lower than two weeks before Black Friday, day by day, 2024 and 2025. By Black Friday about half were priced higher and about one in five lower; six weeks later about seven in ten were higher.](/media/1200/black-friday-priced-higher-lower-1d68a3ad1c.webp)

By Black Friday, about half were already priced higher and only about 1 in 5 lower. Six weeks later, about 7 in 10 were higher. Both years look almost the same.

Real Black Friday dips were rare. During Black Friday week, only **22 of about 1,000** products (2%) sold for 10% or more below their price two weeks earlier in 2024, and **53 of about 1,350** (4%) in 2025.

## Booster boxes and ETBs went up the most

In 2024, the products people actually buy as gifts climbed the most:

- **Elite Trainer Boxes:** +3% on Black Friday, +10% two weeks later, **+18%** by mid-January
- **Booster boxes:** +1% on Black Friday, +4% two weeks later, **+13%** by mid-January
- **Booster bundles:** +9% on Black Friday, **+22%** by mid-January

![2024 median price change by product type on Black Friday, two weeks later and six weeks later: booster bundles +9%, +8%, +22%; Elite Trainer Boxes +3%, +10%, +18%; booster boxes +1%, +4%, +13%; all sealed $20+ +0%, +2%, +6%.](/media/1200/black-friday-2024-by-type-c43ae3a9d7.webp)

Some examples, two weeks before Black Friday 2024 → Black Friday → mid-January:

- **Surging Sparks booster box:** $176 → $210 → $246
- **Surging Sparks ETB:** $49 → $59 → $78
- **Stellar Crown booster box:** $108 → $119 → $159
- **Paldean Fates ETB:** $56 → $67 → $89

And here's where those four are today. Nobody who waited on them got a better price. (These boxes show live TCGplayer market prices and update every day, so they won't match the dated numbers in the text.)

(The run-up to Prismatic Evolutions in January 2025 probably helped push everything up that winter.)

2025 was calmer. ETBs and booster boxes stayed about flat through December, and booster bundles drifted down a few percent. But even in a quiet year, Black Friday itself wasn't the cheap day:

- **Mega Evolution booster box:** $223 two weeks before → **$257** on Black Friday → $248 in January
- **Journey Together booster box:** $237 → $238 → $248
- **Destined Rivals booster box:** $383 → $397 → $402

## The exception: brand-new sets

The things that did get cheaper over the holidays were new releases settling down, and that happens whatever the date. Phantasmal Flames came out two weeks before Black Friday 2025. Its booster box went from $287 to $278 on Black Friday and $221 by Christmas, then came back up. (Same pattern I found for [Delta Reign](/articles/delta-reign-the-first-two-weeks).)

## But TCGplayer's Cyber Weekend still counts

The prices don't drop, but TCGplayer runs its own promotion over the holiday weekend, and it's back this year: **10% back in TCGplayer store credit on everything**, across every game, from 9 a.m. Eastern on Black Friday, November 27, to 11 p.m. Eastern on Cyber Monday, November 30. Subscribers get 13% or more.

So it's not a sale in the "prices are lower" sense. But if you were going to buy that box anyway, buying it during Cyber Weekend gets you the same price you'd have paid the week before, plus credit toward your next order. That's a discount, just a delayed one.

## So when should you buy?

- **Buying gifts on TCGplayer?** Buy during Cyber Weekend (November 27 to 30) at the latest, and don't wait past it. The price won't be any lower than the week before, but you'll get 10% back in store credit. Waiting until later in December didn't save money either year, and in 2024 waiting just two weeks past Black Friday cost about 10% on ETBs.
- **The real Black Friday deals are at retail.** Big stores sometimes drop ETBs and tins to MSRP or below for a day. Those sales don't show up in the market price, and they sell out fast, but if you see one, that's the deal.
- **Want a new set?** Black Friday doesn't matter. Give it a couple of months and the singles and most sealed will come down on their own.
