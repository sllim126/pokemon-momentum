Every year around now, everyone's wondering if they should wait for Black Friday to buy that booster box or Elite Trainer Box. It's a fair question. Everything else goes on sale, so why not Pokémon sealed products (on the secondary market, of course)?

So, with the daily TCGplayer prices I have going back to early 2024, I decided to check what actually happened to sealed products around the last two Black Fridays (November 29, 2024 and November 28, 2025).

*TCGplayer's market price is based on what things actually sold for, so if Black Friday made sealed product cheaper across the board, it would show up here. I compared each product's price two weeks before Black Friday with Black Friday itself and the six weeks after. English sealed products worth $20 or more: 879 products in 2024, 1,261 in 2025. Product names link to TCGplayer; those are affiliate links, so Poke6s may earn a small commission if you buy through them.*

## Short answer: no

Prices didn't drop on Black Friday. For most products they had already started creeping up, and they kept going afterwards.

Here's how I checked. I took every sealed product's price two weeks before Black Friday, then looked at it again every day after that: was it selling for more, the same, or less? This is 2025:

![2025, day by day: the share of sealed products selling for more, the same or less than two weeks before Black Friday. On Black Friday about 5 in 10 cost more, 3 in 10 the same and 2 in 10 less; six weeks later about 7 in 10 cost more and 2 in 10 less.](/media/1200/black-friday-2025-more-same-less-2e833b24ea.webp)

Pick any day and read straight up. On the far left almost everything is gray, because that's the starting price. Then, day by day, more products move into orange (more expensive) than into blue (cheaper).

On Black Friday 2025, out of every 10 products:

- about **5** cost more than two weeks earlier
- about **3** hadn't moved
- only **2** cost less

Six weeks later, 7 cost more and still only about 2 cost less. 2024 looked almost exactly the same.

**How much more?** Take the product in the middle (half went up more, half less):

- **2024:** +0.1% on Black Friday, +2% two weeks later, **+6%** by mid-January
- **2025:** +0.3% on Black Friday, +2% two weeks later, **+4%** by mid-January

So on Black Friday itself the typical price had barely moved. It's waiting until after that costs you.

And the big markdowns people hope for? Rare. During Black Friday week, only **22 of about 1,000** products (2%) sold for at least 10% less than two weeks earlier in 2024, and **53 of about 1,350** (4%) in 2025.

## The gift boxes went up the most

In 2024, the products people actually buy as gifts climbed the most:

- **Elite Trainer Boxes:** +3% on Black Friday, +10% two weeks later, **+18%** by mid-January
- **Booster boxes:** +1% on Black Friday, +4% two weeks later, **+13%** by mid-January
- **Booster bundles:** +9% on Black Friday, +8% two weeks later, **+22%** by mid-January

![2024 median price change by product type on Black Friday, two weeks later and six weeks later: booster bundles +9%, +8%, +22%; Elite Trainer Boxes +3%, +10%, +18%; booster boxes +1%, +4%, +13%; all sealed $20+ +0%, +2%, +6%.](/media/1200/black-friday-2024-by-type-c43ae3a9d7.webp)

Some examples, two weeks before Black Friday 2024 → Black Friday → mid-January:

- **[Surging Sparks booster box](tcgplayer:565606):** $176 → $210 → $246

- **[Stellar Crown booster box](tcgplayer:557354):** $108 → $119 → $159
- **[Paldean Fates ETB](tcgplayer:528040):** $56 → $67 → $89

And here's where those four are today. Nobody who waited on them got a better price. (These boxes show live TCGplayer market prices and update every day, so they won't match the dated numbers in the text.)

2025 was calmer. ETBs and booster boxes stayed about flat through December, and booster bundles drifted down a few percent. But even in a quiet year, Black Friday itself wasn't the cheap day:

- **[Mega Evolution booster box](tcgplayer:644298):** $223 two weeks before → **$257** on Black Friday → $248 in January
- **[Journey Together booster box](tcgplayer:610931):** $237 → $238 → $248
- **[Destined Rivals booster box](tcgplayer:624679):** $383 → $397 → $402

## What did get cheaper

Not everything went up. About 1 in 4 products cost less on Black Friday than two weeks before. Most of those were only a dollar or two off, and about half were back above their old price by January. But a few kinds of product really did drop:

- **Brand-new sets settling down.** Phantasmal Flames came out two weeks before Black Friday 2025. Its [Pokémon Center ETB](tcgplayer:654135) went from **$389 to $195**, its 3-pack blisters from about $45 to $24, and its [booster box](tcgplayer:654137) from $287 to $278 on Black Friday and $221 by Christmas, before coming back up. (Same pattern I found for [Delta Reign](/articles/delta-reign-the-first-two-weeks).)
- **Pokémon Center ETBs.** In 2025, [Stellar Crown's](tcgplayer:557340) went from $191 to $119 and [Mega Evolution's](tcgplayer:648415) from $209 to $173.
- **Big premium collections.** The [Prismatic Evolutions Premium Figure Collection](tcgplayer:650799) went from **$229 to $126**, [Team Rocket's Moltres ex Ultra-Premium Collection](tcgplayer:656997) from $278 to $220, and the [Mega Charizard X ex Ultra-Premium Collection](tcgplayer:654213) from $222 to $183.
- **In 2024, odds and ends.** Older theme decks, Build & Battle Boxes and 3-pack blisters from past sets, mostly a few dollars off. Not the usual gifts: only 7 of 40 booster boxes and 13 of 93 ETBs were cheaper that year.

None of these were Black Friday sales. Most of them kept falling for weeks afterwards. So if you're hunting for a deal, look at the newest set's premium products. They do come down, just not because it's Black Friday.

## But TCGplayer's Cyber Weekend still counts

The prices don't drop, but TCGplayer runs its own promotion over the holiday weekend, and it's back this year: **10% back in TCGplayer store credit on everything**, across every game, from 9 a.m. Eastern on Black Friday, November 27, to 11 p.m. Eastern on Cyber Monday, November 30. Subscribers get 13% or more.

So it's not a sale in the "prices are lower" sense. But if you were going to buy that box anyway, buying it during Cyber Weekend gets you the same price you'd have paid the week before, plus credit toward your next order. That's a discount, just a delayed one.

## So when should you buy?

- **Buying gifts on TCGplayer?** Buy by the end of Cyber Weekend (November 27 to 30). The price won't be any lower than the week before, but you'll get 10% back in store credit. Waiting until later in December didn't save money either year, and in 2024 waiting just two weeks past Black Friday cost about 10% on ETBs.
- **The real Black Friday deals are at retail.** Big stores sometimes will have product at MSRP, if it hasn't been bought out yet. Those sales don't show up in the market price, and they sell out fast, but if you see anything at MSRP, that's the deal. We're still deep in scalper territory, and unless you're willing to put in the time (waiting overnight or standing in line for hours), what you'll find on the shelves may be limited. It's getting better, but slowly.
- **Want a new set?** Black Friday doesn't matter. Give it a couple of months and the singles and most sealed will come down on their own.
