Every year around now, someone asks if they should wait for Black Friday to buy that booster box or Elite Trainer Box. It's a fair question. Everything else goes on sale, so why not Pokémon?

I have daily TCGplayer prices going back to early 2024, so I checked what actually happened to sealed products around the last two Black Fridays (November 29, 2024 and November 28, 2025).

*TCGplayer's market price is based on what things actually sold for, so if Black Friday made sealed product cheaper across the board, it would show up here. I compared each product's price two weeks before Black Friday with Black Friday itself and the six weeks after. English sealed products worth $20 or more: 879 products in 2024, 1,261 in 2025.*

## Short answer: no

For the typical sealed product, the price on Black Friday was about the same as two weeks earlier: **+0.1%** in 2024 and **+0.3%** in 2025. Then it kept going up.

**2024, the typical product:**

- Black Friday: +0.1%
- Two weeks later: +2%
- Six weeks later (mid-January): +6%

**2025, the typical product:**

- Black Friday: +0.3%
- Two weeks later: +2%
- Six weeks later: +4%

Real Black Friday dips were rare. During Black Friday week, only **22 of about 1,000** products (2%) sold for 10% or more below their price two weeks earlier in 2024, and **53 of about 1,350** (4%) in 2025.

## Booster boxes and ETBs went up the most

In 2024, the products people actually buy as gifts climbed the most:

- **Elite Trainer Boxes:** +3% on Black Friday, +10% two weeks later, **+18%** by mid-January
- **Booster boxes:** +1% on Black Friday, +4% two weeks later, **+13%** by mid-January
- **Booster bundles:** +9% on Black Friday, **+22%** by mid-January

Some examples, two weeks before Black Friday 2024 → Black Friday → mid-January:

- **Surging Sparks booster box:** $176 → $210 → $246
- **Surging Sparks ETB:** $49 → $59 → $78
- **Stellar Crown booster box:** $108 → $119 → $159
- **Paldean Fates ETB:** $56 → $67 → $89

(The run-up to Prismatic Evolutions in January 2025 probably helped push everything up that winter.)

2025 was calmer. ETBs and booster boxes stayed about flat through December, and booster bundles drifted down a few percent. But even in a quiet year, Black Friday itself wasn't the cheap day:

- **Mega Evolution booster box:** $223 two weeks before → **$257** on Black Friday → $248 in January
- **Journey Together booster box:** $237 → $238 → $248
- **Destined Rivals booster box:** $383 → $397 → $402

## The exception: brand-new sets

The things that did get cheaper over the holidays were new releases settling down, and that happens whatever the date. Phantasmal Flames came out two weeks before Black Friday 2025. Its booster box went from $287 to $278 on Black Friday and $221 by Christmas, then came back up. (Same pattern I found for [Delta Reign](/articles/delta-reign-the-first-two-weeks).)

## So when should you buy?

- **Buying gifts on TCGplayer?** Buy before Thanksgiving, not after. Waiting for Black Friday didn't save money either year, and in 2024 waiting just two weeks past Black Friday cost about 10% on ETBs.
- **The real Black Friday deals are at retail.** Big stores sometimes drop ETBs and tins to MSRP or below for a day. Those sales don't show up in the market price, and they sell out fast, but if you see one, that's the deal.
- **Want a new set?** Black Friday doesn't matter. Give it a couple of months and the singles and most sealed will come down on their own.
