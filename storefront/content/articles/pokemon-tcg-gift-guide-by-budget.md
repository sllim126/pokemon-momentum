If someone on your list is into Pokémon cards and you're not, the shelf (or the TCGplayer search page) is a lot. Same set, six different boxes, prices all over the place.

So here's a guide by budget, using what things actually sell for right now. It's also Cyber Weekend, so if you buy on TCGplayer through Monday night you get 10% back in store credit. (More on that in [the Black Friday post](/articles/is-pokemon-sealed-cheaper-on-black-friday).)

*Prices in the text are TCGplayer market prices as of November 20. The boxes with pictures show today's market price and update every day.*

## First, the one tip that saves the most

**Buy the newer, regular sets.** Right now that's Perfect Order, Chaos Rising, Pitch Black and Delta Reign. They're in print, easy to find, and cost about **$5 to $8 a pack** whatever box they come in.

The special sets (Prismatic Evolutions, Black Bolt, White Flare, Ascended Heroes) and anything with a Charizard on the front (Phantasmal Flames) cost **two to three times as much per pack**. They're great sets, but you're paying for the hype, and the person opening them gets the same number of cards.

Here's what a pack costs in each kind of box, for the four newest regular sets:

- **Booster box** (36 packs): **$4.70 to $5.40** a pack, the cheapest way to buy packs
- **Booster pack** on its own: **$5.40 to $6.00**
- **Booster bundle** (6 packs): **$5.60 to $6.70** a pack
- **Elite Trainer Box** (9 packs, plus sleeves, dice, dividers and the box): **$7.10 to $7.90** a pack

The ETB costs more per pack because you're paying for the extras. For a gift, that's often worth it. The box is part of the fun.

## Under $10: stocking stuffers

A single booster pack. Newer regular sets are **$5 to $6**. A Phantasmal Flames pack is about $12, if they really want a shot at the Charizard.

## Under $25

- **3-pack blisters** (three packs and a promo card): Perfect Order **$21**, Delta Reign **$22**.
- **Mega Heroes mini tins** (a couple of packs, a coin and a tin to keep cards in): about **$21 to $25**. Pick the Pokémon they like.

## $25 to $50

- **Booster bundles** (6 packs): Perfect Order **$34**, Chaos Rising **$35**, Delta Reign **$40**. The best value in this range.
- **Build & Battle Box** (a ready-to-play 40-card deck plus 4 packs): about **$37**. The best pick for a kid who wants to actually *play* the game, not just open packs.
- **Prismatic Evolutions mini tins** (about **$33 to $42**). For the Eevee fan. You're paying the Prismatic premium, but each tin has a different Eeveelution on it, and that matters to the right person.

[[market 672396 712097 672400 593459]]

## $50 to $100

- **Elite Trainer Box**: the classic Pokémon gift. Perfect Order **$64**, Pitch Black **$67**, Chaos Rising **$67**, Delta Reign **$71**. Nine packs, sleeves, dice, card dividers and a sturdy box to keep it all in.
- **A Japanese booster box** for the collector who has everything: 30 packs of Japanese cards (5 cards a pack, not 10) for about **$74 to $83** (Ninja Spinner, Abyss Eye, Nihil Zero, Storm Emeralda). Japanese cards look great in a binder, and the boxes are cheaper than English ones. [We carry Ninja Spinner boxes](/p/pokemon-ninja-spinner-booster-box-japanese) if you want one.

## $100 to $200

- **A booster box**: Perfect Order **$169**, Pitch Black **$172**, Chaos Rising **$179**, Delta Reign **$194**. 36 packs, the cheapest price per pack, and a whole evening of opening.
- **Prismatic Evolutions ETB**, about **$114**. The most-wanted ETB of the last two years, and down from over $200 in June.
- **Pokémon Center ETBs** (only sold by the Pokémon Center: more packs, a promo card): Perfect Order **$102**, Chaos Rising **$111**.

## Going all out

- **Phantasmal Flames booster box**, about **$372**. The Mega Charizard X set. If the person you're shopping for has ever said "I just want the Charizard," this is the one. (Odds are they won't pull it. Don't tell them that.)
- **Prismatic Evolutions Super-Premium Collection**, about **$239**. Packs, promos and a lot of Eevee.

## A few more tips

- **Check the price before you buy at a store.** Some popular boxes sell for more on the shelf than on TCGplayer, and some sell for a lot less. A quick look at the market price takes ten seconds.
- **Don't buy sealed product as an investment gift.** Some boxes go up, a lot don't. Buy what they'll enjoy opening.
- **Not sure what they collect?** Ask what Pokémon they like. A mini tin or a 3-pack blister with their favorite on the front beats a fancier box of something they don't care about.
