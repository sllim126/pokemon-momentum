30th Celebration came out on September 16, the big anniversary set, and for its first couple of weeks it sold for a lot more than it does now. Almost three months later, it's a different picture.

**Short answer:** nearly everything is cheaper. The Elite Trainer Box is down 30% from its second week, the booster bundle a third, and most of the top cards have lost more than half their value. None of the special sets since Prismatic Evolutions fell this fast.

*Prices are TCGplayer market prices as of November 25, 2026, compared with each product's price in the set's second week (around September 30), once the release-day chaos had settled. The boxes below show today's prices and update every day. Product names link to TCGplayer; those are affiliate links, so Poke6s may earn a small commission if you buy through them.*

The mini tins and the big collection boxes fell hardest. Every one of the ten mini tins is now between $21 and $25, less than half what they sold for at launch.

A few things held up. The ex tins are about where they started ([Sylveon ex](tcgplayer:704149) $73, [Greninja ex](tcgplayer:704150) $57), and the 2-pack blister went *up*, from $51 at release to $63.

The fall has slowed down, though. Over the last ten days of the data, the ETB, the booster bundle and the packs barely moved.

Above all of those sit the three Mew "RGB" cards: monochrome holo Mews in red, green and blue, and incredibly rare. They're selling for $3,100 to $4,300 each, and the red one was $6,000 in the second week. With so few copies around, those prices rest on very few sales.

## The Classic Collection

The Classic Collection reprints famous cards from the last 30 years, and it fell even harder than the main set. [Lugia](tcgplayer:714386) went from $307 to $117, [Charizard](tcgplayer:714372) from $195 to $105, and [Magikarp](tcgplayer:716210) from $125 to $59. The 30 Classic Collection cards added together were worth about $1,100 in the second week. Now they're worth about $515.

## Japanese: the same story

The [Japanese 30th Celebration booster box](tcgplayer:718620) went from $203 in its second week to $141 (−31%), its lowest price yet. The Japanese Charizard dropped from $192 to $127, and the special art rares are down 59 to 69%, a little more than in English.

## How that compares with other special sets

Over the same first ten weeks, measured the same way:

| Elite Trainer Box | Second week | Ten weeks in | Change |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Prismatic Evolutions (Jan 2025) | $103 | $137 | +34% |
| Black Bolt (Jul 2025) | $84 | $86 | +2% |
| White Flare (Jul 2025) | $81 | $79 | −2% |
| Ascended Heroes (Jan 2026) | $137 | $134 | −2% |
| **30th Celebration (Sep 2026)** | **$162** | **$113** | **−30%** |

None of the others fell anywhere near this much. But a slow start didn't decide where they ended up. Black Bolt and White Flare sat flat for six months, then nearly doubled by their first birthday (the ETBs are $149 and $137 today). Ascended Heroes dipped in its first month, climbed to $178 by month four, and is back to $128.

## Why it fell

Honestly? I think it's three things together: more product, easier ways to get it, and high hit rates.

- **Pokémon is printing this to the ground.** They want anyone and everyone to have access, and there's a lot of 30th Celebration product, in a lot of different boxes. Months after release, it's still out there.
- **Big stores are drawing names instead of racing bots.** Walmart started selling the most wanted products by random drawing at the end of August, 30th Celebration included ([Dexerto](https://www.dexerto.com/pokemon/pokemon-fans-now-need-lottery-luck-to-buy-cards-at-walmart-3404656/)), and Target has reportedly started doing the same ([Screen Rant](https://screenrant.com/pokemon-tcg-lottery-target-enter-rules/)). More boxes end up with collectors instead of resellers.
- **The hits are easy to pull.** When opening a few packs gets you the cards you want, nobody needs to buy them on the secondary market.
- **The hype moved on.** Everyone's attention went to Delta Reign, so there's not much resale demand left for 30th Celebration.

Put together, that makes this an easy set to complete, and patience is paying off. The launch prices were the top, not the start of a climb.

## So is it time to buy?

- **If you want to open it:** it's a lot cheaper than it was. A booster pack is still about $9.50, nearly double a pack from a regular set, so you're still paying for the anniversary.
- **If you want the cards:** buying singles is cheaper than ever for this set. Mew ex for $58 is a very different decision from Mew ex for $139.
- **If you're hoping it'll climb back:** maybe. Black Bolt and White Flare show that a special set can sit flat and then take off. But nothing in this set's first three months points that way yet, and that's no reason to buy something you don't want to open or keep.
