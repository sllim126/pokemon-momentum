A booster box is the cheapest way to buy packs, but which one? There are four English sets in print, a stack of Japanese ones, and the Japanese boxes cost less than half as much. So I compared what each box costs per pack and per card, and how its price has moved since release.

**Short answer:** the cheapest English box right now is **[Chaos Rising](tcgplayer:684444), at $166**. The cheapest Japanese box is **[Abyss Eye](tcgplayer:695112), at $71**. Both are at the lowest price they've ever been, and once you count the cards, they cost almost exactly the same: about **47¢ a card**.

*Prices in the text are TCGplayer market prices as of November 25, 2026. The boxes below show today's prices and update every day. Product names link to TCGplayer; those are affiliate links, so Poke6s may earn a small commission if you buy through them.*

## What's in each box

The two boxes aren't the same size, which is why the Japanese one looks so cheap:

- **English booster box:** 36 packs of 10 cards, **360 cards**.
- **Japanese booster box:** 30 packs of 5 cards, **150 cards**.

So a Japanese pack costs about half an English pack, but it has half the cards. Per card, they come out even.

## English: the four sets in print

- **[Chaos Rising](tcgplayer:684444):** $166, $4.60 a pack, 46¢ a card. Down 31% since its second week.
- **[Pitch Black](tcgplayer:692939):** $171, $4.75 a pack, 47¢ a card. Down 9% since its second week.
- **[Perfect Order](tcgplayer:672394):** $174, $4.83 a pack, 48¢ a card. Down 16% since its second week.
- **[Delta Reign](tcgplayer:712094):** $191, $5.31 a pack, 53¢ a card. About the same as its second week.

Chaos Rising sold for $240 two weeks after it came out in May. It's down to $166, the lowest it's been. Delta Reign is only three weeks old and still costs the most, but it's barely moved since launch.

The special sets cost a lot more. A [Phantasmal Flames](tcgplayer:654137) box is $363 and a Mega Evolution box $293, about $8 to $10 a pack.

Three more Japanese boxes cost more per card:

- **[Inferno X](tcgplayer:655968)** (the Mega Charizard X set, 30 packs of 5): $117, $3.90 a pack, 78¢ a card. The same Charizard premium as Phantasmal Flames in English, just smaller.
- **[Mega Dream ex](tcgplayer:666254)** (a high class set: 10 packs of 10 cards): $76, $7.63 a pack, 76¢ a card. Down a third since its second week and at its lowest price yet.
- **[30th Celebration](tcgplayer:718620)** (20 packs of 6 cards): $141, $7.03 a pack, $1.17 a card. It's down from $203 in its second week, also its lowest yet, but still the most expensive per card by a long way.

## When to buy: Japanese boxes get cheaper, English ones haven't lately

**Japanese boxes:** for 15 sets since December 2024, the typical box was 11% below its second-week price two months after release, and about 80% of them were cheaper by then. Most hit their lowest price somewhere between month 2 and month 6. If a new Japanese set just came out, waiting a couple of months has usually paid off.

**English boxes:** it depends on the year.

- **The 2024 sets tripled.** Temporal Forces, Twilight Masquerade and Stellar Crown all sold for about $100 a box in their first months. Today they're $311 to $328.
- **The 2025 and 2026 sets mostly haven't.** Journey Together sold for $228 in its second week and is $199 now. Mega Evolution is about where it started. Perfect Order, Chaos Rising and Pitch Black are all below their second-week prices.

The price data can't say why. What it does say: buying an English box now and waiting isn't the sure thing it looked like in 2024.

## So which is the best deal?

- **For the most cards for your money:** Chaos Rising or Abyss Eye, at about 46¢ and 47¢ a card. Call it a tie.
- **For the cards themselves:** pick the language you collect. The prices don't decide it.
- **For a gift:** an English box. Ten cards a pack, and the person opening it can read them.
- **For a binder:** Japanese. Plenty of collectors prefer how they look, and the box costs less than half as much up front.
- **Waiting for a deal:** the new Japanese sets are the ones that drop. Don't count on an English box dropping much, and don't count on one tripling either.

*About the data: TCGplayer market prices for booster boxes (not cases, half boxes or enhanced boxes), English from February 2024 and Japanese from December 2024. "Second week" is each box's price 14 days after its set came out. Per-card prices are the box price divided by the cards in the box: 360 for English, 150 for regular Japanese sets, 100 for Mega Dream ex and 120 for 30th Celebration.*
