## What we keep

When you place an order we keep your **name, email address, shipping address and order
history** (what you bought, what you paid, and shipping and tracking details).

## Why

To ship your order, send order and shipping emails, handle returns and refunds, and keep the
business and tax records the law requires.

## Payments

Payments are processed by [Stripe](https://stripe.com/privacy). Your card details go straight
to Stripe and never reach our servers.

## How long

Order records are kept for as long as tax law requires (currently up to seven years), then
deleted.

## Email list

If you join our email list, we keep your email address, the page you signed up from and the
date. We use it only to send Poke6s news: new stock, restocks and sales. We never sell or share
it. Every email has an unsubscribe link that works in one click, with no sign-in; after that we
keep only a note that your address is unsubscribed, so it isn't emailed again. You can also
email [admin@poke6s.com](mailto:admin@poke6s.com) and we'll remove it.

## Cookies and visit counts

This site sets no tracking cookies and runs no tracking scripts. Your cart is kept in your
own browser.

To see how the site is used, our own server keeps daily totals: how many people visited,
which pages were viewed, the kind of device (phone, tablet or computer), and the website
that linked you here, if any. To count each person once a day, it makes a scrambled code
from your IP address and browser. That code is deleted at the end of the day and can't be
traced back to you, and your IP address isn't stored with the totals. The totals are kept
for about a year.

Traffic to this site passes through [Cloudflare](https://www.cloudflare.com/privacypolicy/),
which protects it and delivers the pages. Cloudflare sees your IP address to do that.

## Your choices

Email [admin@poke6s.com](mailto:admin@poke6s.com) to see, correct or delete the information we
hold about you, apart from records we must keep for tax purposes.
