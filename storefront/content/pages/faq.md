## Is everything sealed and authentic?

Yes. Sealed product is sold factory sealed, as received from our distributors and
importers. Japanese product is imported from Japan.

## What does the "market price" on a product page mean?

It's the recent TCGplayer market price for the same product, tracked daily on
[market.poke6s.com](https://market.poke6s.com). Click through to see its price history.

## Do you ship outside the US?

Not yet. We ship within the United States only.

## When will my order ship?

See [Shipping](/shipping) for handling times and carriers.

## Can I return something?

Unopened sealed product in its original wrap can be returned within 14 days. See
[Returns & cancellations](/returns).

## Do you sell singles?

Singles are sold through our [TCGplayer store](https://poke6s.tcgplayerpro.com/).
