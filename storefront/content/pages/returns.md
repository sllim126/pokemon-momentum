## Returns

- Sealed product can be returned **unopened, in its original wrap, within 14 days** of
  delivery.
- Opened product, or product with damaged or removed shrink wrap, can't be returned.
- **We inspect every returned item before refunding.** The refund is issued once the item
  has arrived and passed inspection, to the original payment method.
- Return shipping is paid by the buyer, unless we made a mistake with your order or it
  arrived damaged.

To start a return, email [admin@poke6s.com](mailto:admin@poke6s.com) with your order number.
Please don't send items back without contacting us first.

## Cancellations

You can cancel an order that hasn't shipped yet by emailing us with your order number. Once an
order has shipped, it's handled as a return.
